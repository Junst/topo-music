## What the table is for

**The controls fix the scale, and two of them are load-bearing.** An analytic
circle of fifths scores ~1 in every column, so a null elsewhere is a null of the
representation. Random features carry nothing and show nothing. The third is the
one that matters: orthogonal per-key codes decode key **better than any real
representation** (.651) with classes equidistant by construction, and their
weight geometry is −0.005. So rho_5 W is not something an accurate 24-way key
classifier produces automatically on this label set -- the sceptical reading
that keys a fifth apart share six of seven scale degrees and are therefore
confusable *whatever* was fitted is ruled out.

**Column 2 vs column 4 is the paper's dissociation.** log-CQT has the highest
key accuracy of any real arm (.492) and *no* fifths structure in its centroids
(−0.035, p = .52), while its probe weights carry that structure strongly
(+0.560, z = +9.1). Tonal relations are linearly accessible in a log-spectrogram
and simply absent from its metric.

**The fold × norm block refutes the simple story.** Neither octave folding nor
per-frame normalisation alone produces fifths geometry: `cqt_fold` −0.106 and
`cqt_norm` −0.117, against `chroma` +0.475. Worse for the simple story, each
change *alone* produces **chromatic** structure instead (+0.391 and +0.491),
which is the opposite organisation. The two operations only yield the circle of
fifths in conjunction, so "octave folding is what separates chroma from CQT" is
not supportable and is not claimed.

**What MERT does is move structure between the columns, not create it.** Its
readout geometry (+0.41 to +0.48) is *lower* than log-CQT's, EnCodec's or
chroma's, while its centroid geometry (+0.29 to +0.47) is far higher. Depth then
trades it back: rho_5 cent falls 0.445 -> 0.294 from L4 to L24 while rho_mode
rises 0.216 -> 0.328. rho_mode is a caution rather than a finding -- it is
−0.054 and not significant in the chromagram, so major/minor is not separating
these centroids through pitch-class content, and in an all-EDM corpus
production and instrumentation are the obvious alternative.

**rho_chr stays near zero for every MERT layer** (−0.025 to +0.049), so the
fifths result is not a generic "nearby keys look alike" effect.
